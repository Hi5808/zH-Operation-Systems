# Blackview BL6000 Pro 5G (MT6873) — Ubuntu Touch port (codename HI001)

Sanitized public tree. **Binaries are intentionally stripped** (kernel modules, plugins,
boot images): rebuild them with `build.sh`. **No vendor binaries are redistributed** — this
holds only our own source, patches, overlays, and derived reverse-engineering notes.
Kernel work is GPLv2 (MediaTek/Xiaomi 4.14 base + our device drivers/patches). Community
"as-is" port; see the UBports devices page (codename HI001) and the RE guide for context.
